# Pecan County Order No. 2026-14 (FICTIONAL eval fixture, as adopted, 5 pages)
## Large-Load Facility Siting Standards

**Sec. 1. Purpose.** To protect public health, safety, and property values while allowing lawful development of large-load facilities (any facility with an interconnection request of 75 MW or more) in unincorporated Pecan County, under the authority of Texas Local Government Code §240.

**Sec. 2. Definitions.** "Large-load facility" means a data center, cryptocurrency mine, or industrial facility requesting 75 MW or more of interconnection. "Sensitive receptor" means an occupied residence, school, church, or hospital.

**Sec. 3. Setbacks.** A large-load facility's buildings and outdoor mechanical equipment shall be set back at least 1,000 feet from any sensitive receptor existing on the date of the permit application.

**Sec. 4. Noise.** Sound measured at the property line of any sensitive receptor shall not exceed 55 dBA between 7 a.m. and 10 p.m., or 50 dBA between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m. Measurement follows ANSI S12.9. The operator shall submit a third-party sound study before the permit is issued and a verification study within 180 days after operation begins.

**Sec. 5. Water.** Applicants shall disclose the cooling technology, the projected annual water consumption, and the water source. Facilities using closed-loop cooling are exempt from the annual reporting requirement in Sec. 7. Nothing in this order limits groundwater rights under Texas Water Code Chapter 36.

**Sec. 6. Decommissioning.** The operator shall post a decommissioning bond equal to 110% of the engineer-estimated cost of removing above-ground equipment. The bond is released after removal is certified.

**Sec. 7. Annual report.** Operators of non-exempt facilities shall file an annual report with the County Judge stating actual water consumed and any noise complaints received and their resolution.

**Sec. 8. Enforcement.** A violation is a Class C misdemeanor, each day a separate offense, maximum fine $500 per day. The County Attorney may seek injunctive relief.

**Sec. 9. Applicability.** This order does not apply to facilities that submitted a complete permit application before the effective date. It does not regulate electricity rates, which are set by the PUCT and ERCOT.

**Sec. 10. Effective date.** Effective 30 days after adoption. Adopted September 8, 2026, by a 4-1 vote of Commissioners Court.
