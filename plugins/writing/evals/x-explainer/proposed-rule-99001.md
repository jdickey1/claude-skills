# Lone Star Water Commission, Project No. 99001 (FICTIONAL eval fixture, proposed rule)
## Proposed 30 TAC §301.40, Large Industrial Water Users. Published September 12, 2026.

Implements House Bill 1234 (fictional), which directed the Commission to set standards for new industrial water users.

**(a) Applicability.** Applies to any new facility requesting more than 500,000 gallons per day of fresh surface or groundwater. Data centers, hydrogen plants, and chip fabs are named examples. Facilities using only reclaimed, produced, or brackish water are exempt.

**(b) Water security deposit.** Before a supply contract is approved, the applicant posts security of $20 per gallon of requested daily capacity. A 1-million-gallon-per-day request posts $20 million.

**(c) Forfeiture.** If actual use stays below 40% of requested capacity for 24 consecutive months, the applicant forfeits 75% of the security to the regional water authority, and the unused capacity returns to the regional allocation.

**(d) Refund.** The full deposit is returned after five consecutive years of use at or above 80% of requested capacity.

**(e) Drought curtailment.** During a Stage 3 drought declaration, covered facilities cut fresh-water use by 25% before any residential restriction takes effect.

**(f) Public disclosure.** The applicant's legal name, parent company, cooling technology, and requested capacity are posted publicly within 10 days of filing. No confidential filings.

**(g) Comment period.** Comments are due November 14, 2026. Any person may file. A public hearing is set for October 28, 2026, in Austin.
